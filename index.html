The Assignment

A Bank With Two True Stories

Maendeleo Bank was founded in 2008 by the Evangelical Lutheran Church in Tanzania, Eastern and Coastal Diocese. That heritage is not a footnote. It is the reason a generation of customers trust you in a market where trust is the hardest thing to earn.

At the same time, you are a full commercial bank, listed on the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange since inception, working toward half a trillion shillings in total assets by 2030. Investors, regulators, and mid-sized corporate treasurers will judge you on entirely commercial terms.

Lean too far into either story and the other one gets quieter than it should. That is the tension named in your brief, and it is the first thing we want to answer, before we talk about content calendars or channels.
Section 11, In Our Own Words

One Identity, Not Two

The two stories are not in competition.

Trust, discipline, and long-term relationship, the same qualities that made a diocese comfortable putting its name behind a bank in 2008, are exactly what a DSE investor or a mid-sized corporate treasurer is looking for. The work is not to balance two identities. It is to speak from the one identity that already contains both: a bank that treats banking as a relationship, not a transaction.

What changes across audiences is proof, not values.

An investor sees NPL ratios and asset trajectory. A congregation sees the same discipline as stewardship and continuity. A business client sees it as reliability and speed. Together in Progress holds across all of it, because it was never only a community-bank tagline.

We will sequence the proof.

Early content leans into commercial credibility, since that is the newer story investors and corporates need repeating. Heritage never disappears, it becomes depth and differentiation once commercial seriousness is established.

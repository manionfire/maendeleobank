Engagement Models & Investment

We have not been asked to guess which model you prefer, so here is our honest read on both, side by side, plus indicative costs across all three response areas.

Option A — Retainer

What it means
Flago runs your social media and creative output on an ongoing monthly retainer, as your standing agency partner.
Best suited to
Consistent voice and fast response without building an internal team.
Trade-off
Higher ongoing cost, and dependency on an external partner for something increasingly core to how the bank operates.

Option B — Foundation, Then Handover

What it means
Flago builds the brand voice, content system, templates, and a working calendar, trains an internal team, then hands over day-to-day execution, remaining available for creative and events.
Best suited to
Building lasting internal capability and lowering cost over time.
Trade-off
Real execution risk during and after handover if the internal team is under-resourced, unless a maintenance cadence survives it.
Our Recommendation

Option B, with a standing creative and events retainer alongside it. As a public, DSE-listed company working toward half a trillion shillings by 2030, owning your own day-to-day voice is where you should be heading. We'd rather build you toward that than make you dependent on us indefinitely.

The Assignment

A Bank With Two True Stories

Maendeleo Bank was founded in 2008 by the Evangelical Lutheran Church in Tanzania, Eastern and Coastal Diocese. That heritage is not a footnote. It is the reason a generation of customers trust you in a market where trust is the hardest thing to earn.

At the same time, you are a full commercial bank, listed on the Dar es Salaam Stock Exchange since inception, with half a trillion shillings in total assets as your target by 2030. Investors, regulators, and mid-sized corporate treasurers will judge you on entirely commercial terms.

Lean too far into either story and the other one gets quieter than it should. That is the tension named in your brief, and it is the first thing we want to answer, before we talk about content calendars or channels.
Section 11, In Our Own Words

One Identity, Not Two

The two stories are not in competition.

Trust, discipline, and long-term relationship, the same qualities that made a diocese comfortable putting its name behind a bank in 2008, are exactly what a DSE investor or a mid-sized corporate treasurer is looking for when they choose a banking partner over its competitors. The work is not to balance two identities. It is to speak from the one identity that already contains both: a bank that treats banking as a relationship, not a transaction.

What changes across audiences is proof, not values.

An investor sees NPL ratios, digital CASA growth, and asset trajectory. A congregation sees the same discipline expressed as stewardship and continuity. A business client sees it as reliability and speed. Together in Progress holds across all of it, because it was never only a community-bank tagline. It is a commercial promise that happens to have been proven inside a community first.

We will sequence the proof.

Early content leans into commercial credibility, since that is the newer story and the one investors and corporates need repeating before they engage further. Heritage never disappears. It becomes depth and differentiation once commercial seriousness is established, not the lead story.

Two Ways to Work Together

Engagement Models

We have not been asked to guess which model you prefer, so here is our honest read on both, side by side.

Option A — Retainer

What it means
Flago runs your social media and creative output on an ongoing monthly retainer, as your standing agency partner.
Best suited to
Consistent voice and fast response without building an internal team.
Trade-off
Higher ongoing cost, and dependency on an external partner for something increasingly core to how the bank operates.

Option B — Foundation, Then Handover

What it means
Flago builds the brand voice, content system, templates, and a working calendar, trains an internal team, then hands over day-to-day execution, with Flago remaining available for creative and events.
Best suited to
Building lasting internal capability and lowering cost over time.
Trade-off
Real execution risk during and after handover if the internal team is under-resourced, unless a maintenance cadence survives the handover.
Our Recommendation

Option B, with a standing creative and events retainer alongside it. Your brief already points this way in Section 7, where it names an agency being available for "creative and event work as it comes up" even after core execution moves in-house. As a public, DSE-listed company working toward half a trillion shillings in assets by 2030, owning your own day-to-day voice is where you should be heading. We would rather build you toward that than make you dependent on us indefinitely.
